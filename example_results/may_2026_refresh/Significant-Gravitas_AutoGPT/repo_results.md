## Main findings

Highest-stars project in the sample (184k★, 46k forks). 430 attributable developers but **bus factor 1**, HHI 4,448, **lines-Gini 0.99** — extreme effort concentration despite massive contributor breadth. Top contributor `waynehamadi` does 23% of lines; PR turnaround 0.1 h. Issue first response 22 h, 3,815 issues, 70% closed. Health % is 100. **Summary**: textbook "drive-by celebrity project" — thousands of contributors, but a very small team actually shipping merged code; the dataset's strongest example of star count not predicting contributor distribution.
