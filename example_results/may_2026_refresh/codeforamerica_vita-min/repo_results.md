## Main findings

The Volunteer Income Tax Assistance backend (2019, 50 developers, 1.5M LOC added). Bus factor 4 (one of the most resilient cores in codeforamerica). **Lowest single-contributor week percentage in the new sample (3%)** alongside civiform and AutoGPT — these three are the only repos in the dataset with genuinely distributed weekly contribution. **Summary**: the codeforamerica project that most resembles a sustainable open-source community.
